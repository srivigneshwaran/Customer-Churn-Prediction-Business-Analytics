1. EXECUTIVE PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================
Problem Statement:
A telecommunications provider has 7,043 customers generating over $456,000 in
Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR). Each month, a significant percentage of customers
cancel their subscriptions (churn). Acquiring a new customer costs 5 to 25 times
more than retaining an existing one.

Core Objectives:
- Identify patterns and behavioral drivers causing customer departures.
- Statistically validate whether differences in charges and tenure are significant.
- Predict whether an individual customer will churn using Machine Learning.
- Provide transparent Odds Ratios explaining why a customer is flagged as high risk.
- Write enterprise SQL queries to quantify revenue at risk and extract targeted
  outreach lists.

Technology Stack:
- Python 3.12 (Core programming and data science ecosystem)
- Pandas & NumPy (Data wrangling, type casting, array operations)
- Matplotlib & Seaborn (Statistical visualization, distributions, heatmaps)
- SciPy Stats (Parametric & non-parametric hypothesis tests, Cramer's V, Bayes)
- Scikit-Learn (Data pipelines, scaling, encoding, modeling, evaluation)
- Joblib (Model serialization for production deployment)
- SQL / SQLite / MySQL (Database DDL schema, window functions, cohort analytics)
- Jupyter Notebooks (Interactive development, rich output rendering)

DETAILED RATIONALE FOR EACH MODEL:

1. LOGISTIC REGRESSION WITH BALANCED WEIGHTING (PRIMARY PRODUCTION MODEL):
   - How it works:
     Computes the linear combination of weighted features:
       z = beta_0 + beta_1*X_1 + beta_2*X_2 + ... + beta_k*X_k
     Then passes z through the logistic sigmoid function:
       P(Churn) = 1 / (1 + e^(-z))
   - Why it was selected:
     a) Mathematical Transparency & Odds Ratios:
        Unlike tree ensembles that output obscure feature importance scores,
        Logistic Regression coefficients directly translate to Odds Ratios ($e^\beta$).
        For example:
        * Contract_Month-to-month has beta = +0.765 -> Odds Ratio = 2.15.
          This means Month-to-Month customers have 2.15 times higher odds of
          churning compared to the baseline.
        * Contract_Two year has beta = -1.381 -> Odds Ratio = 0.25.
          This means signing a 2-year contract reduces churn odds by 75%.
     b) Balanced Class Weighting (class_weight='balanced'):
        Because only 26.54% of customers churn, a standard model can easily achieve
        73.5% accuracy by simply predicting "No Churn" for every customer.
        In churn prevention, missing a churning customer (False Negative) costs the
        business hundreds of dollars in lost lifetime value, whereas reaching out
        to a loyal customer (False Positive) costs only an email or discount coupon.
        Balanced weighting penalizes false negatives heavily, achieving 78.61% Recall.
     c) Regulatory & Corporate Governance:
        In large enterprise client accounts (such as telecom or banking),
        models must be auditable, explainable, and compliant with fair lending/pricing.

2. RANDOM FOREST CLASSIFIER (BENCHMARK MODEL):
   - How it works:
     Builds 150 independent decision trees using bagging (bootstrap aggregation)
     and random feature subsets, then averages their probabilistic predictions.
   - Why it was used:
     a) Captures Non-Linear Interactions:
        Tests whether complex multi-feature interactions (e.g., tenure < 6 AND
        charges > $90 AND streaming = Yes) create non-linear risk spikes that
        a linear model might miss.
     b) Benchmark Validation:
        Random Forest achieved 0.8434 ROC-AUC compared to Logistic Regression's
        0.8418 ROC-AUC. The difference is marginal (less than 0.002). This proves
        that the underlying relationships are largely linear log-odds, confirming
        that Logistic Regression is the better enterprise choice due to its simplicity.

3. GRADIENT BOOSTING CLASSIFIER (BENCHMARK MODEL):
   - How it works:
     Builds decision trees sequentially, where each new tree is trained to predict
     the pseudo-residuals (errors) of the preceding trees.
   - Why it was used:
     a) High Precision Baseline:
        Gradient Boosting achieved the highest overall Accuracy (80.34%) and
        Precision (66.11%).
     b) Illustrates the Precision-Recall Trade-off in Business:
        Gradient Boosting's Recall dropped to only 53.21%—meaning it missed
        almost half of all churning customers. This serves as an interview
        talking point demonstrating why raw accuracy is misleading for imbalanced
        business problems.

5. MODULE-BY-MODULE TECHNICAL WALKTHROUGH & KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

MODULE 1: DATA CLEANING & PREPROCESSING
- Input: 7,043 rows, 21 columns.
- Issue Discovered: TotalCharges was stored as type object (string).
- Root Cause Analysis: 11 rows contained empty spaces (' '). All 11 records had
  tenure = 0. These were brand new signups who had not yet completed a billing cycle.
- Solution: Rather than dropping records (which introduces survivorship bias) or
  filling with the global median (which distorts newly onboarded accounts), we
  imputed 0.0, matching real business reality.
- Output: 7,043 rows, 23 columns saved to data/cleaned_telco_churn.csv.

MODULE 2: EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS (EDA)
- Baseline Metrics: 1,869 churned customers (26.54%), 5,174 retained (73.46%).
- Tenure Curve:
  * 0 - 12 Months: 47.4% churn rate (Critical vulnerability window).
  * 13 - 24 Months: 28.7% churn rate.
  * 61 - 72 Months: 6.6% churn rate (High brand loyalty).
- Contract Breakdown:
  * Month-to-month: 42.71% churn (Represents 88.6% of all churn volume).
  * One year: 11.27% churn.
  * Two year: 2.83% churn.
- Internet Service Paradox:
  * Fiber Optic: 41.89% churn (Average bill: $87+).
  * DSL: 18.96% churn (Average bill: $58+).
  * No Internet: 7.40% churn.
- Payment Methods:
  * Electronic check: 45.29% churn.
  * Credit card (automatic): 15.24% churn.
  * Bank transfer (automatic): 16.71% churn.

MODULE 3: STATISTICAL ANALYSIS & HYPOTHESIS TESTING
- Parametric Test 1: Monthly Charges Difference (Welch's t-test)
  * Churned Mean: $74.44 (std: $24.67) | Retained Mean: $61.27 (std: $31.09)
  * Welch's t = 18.40, p-value = 8.59e-73 -> Reject H0.
  * Conclusion: Churners pay significantly higher monthly bills.
- Non-Parametric Test: Mann-Whitney U test on Monthly Charges
  * U = 3,747,404.5, p-value = 1.09e-58 -> Confirmed non-parametric robustness.
- Parametric Test 2: Tenure Difference (Welch's t-test)
  * Retained Mean: 37.57 months | Churned Mean: 17.98 months
  * Welch's t = 34.82, p-value = 7.79e-208 -> Reject H0.
  * Conclusion: Churners leave significantly earlier in their lifecycle.
- Categorical Association (Chi-Square & Cramer's V):
  * Contract: Cramer's V = 0.410 (Strongest predictor)
  * OnlineSecurity: Cramer's V = 0.347 (Strong)
  * TechSupport: Cramer's V = 0.337 (Strong)
  * InternetService: Cramer's V = 0.322 (Strong)
  * PaymentMethod: Cramer's V = 0.301 (Strong)
  * PaperlessBilling: Cramer's V = 0.192 (Moderate)
  * SeniorCitizen: Cramer's V = 0.150 (Moderate)
- Bayesian Probability:
  * P(Churn) prior = 26.54%
  * P(Churn | Month-to-month) = 42.71%
  * P(Churn | M2M + Fiber Optic + Electronic Check + Tenure <= 12) = 71.16%

MODULE 4: MACHINE LEARNING MODELING
- Preprocessing: Numerical features scaled with StandardScaler; categorical features
  encoded with OneHotEncoder(drop='first', sparse_output=False).
- Split: Stratified 80% train (5,634 rows), 20% test (1,409 rows).
- Logistic Regression Evaluation on Test Set:
  * True Positives (Caught churners): 294 out of 374 (78.61% Recall)
  * True Negatives (Retained correctly): 748 out of 1,035 (72.27% Specificity)
  * ROC-AUC: 0.8418
  * PR-AUC: 0.6345
- Top Risk Factors Increasing Churn Odds:
  * InternetService_Fiber optic: Odds Ratio = 2.63 (+163% odds)
  * TotalCharges: Odds Ratio = 1.58 (+58% odds)
  * PaymentMethod_Electronic check: Odds Ratio = 1.49 (+49% odds)
  * PaperlessBilling_Yes: Odds Ratio = 1.40 (+40% odds)
- Top Protective Factors Decreasing Churn Odds:
  * Contract_Two year: Odds Ratio = 0.25 (75% reduction in odds)
  * tenure: Odds Ratio = 0.32 (68% reduction in odds per std dev)
  * Contract_One year: Odds Ratio = 0.49 (51% reduction in odds)
  * TechSupport_Yes: Odds Ratio = 0.73 (27% reduction in odds)
  * OnlineSecurity_Yes: Odds Ratio = 0.68 (32% reduction in odds)

9. INTERVIEW TALKING POINTS & FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (ENTERPRISE DATA SCIENCE)
================================================================================

Q1: Why did you choose Logistic Regression instead of Random Forest or XGBoost?
A1: "In enterprise data science, business stakeholders and executives require
interpretability. Logistic Regression with class-balanced weighting achieved
78.61% Recall and 0.8418 ROC-AUC on our test set—nearly matching Random Forest
(0.8434 ROC-AUC). Crucially, Logistic Regression provides mathematically rigorous
Odds Ratios ($e^\beta$). We can tell leadership that Month-to-Month contracts multiply
churn odds by 2.15x, and Two-Year contracts cut churn odds by 75%. It is fast,
deployable at under 10 KB, and fully auditable."

Q2: Why is Recall prioritized over Accuracy in this business problem?
A2: "The dataset has a class imbalance (73.5% non-churn vs 26.5% churn). A naive
model could predict that no customer ever leaves and achieve 73.5% accuracy, but
it would be useless to the business. A False Negative (missing a churner) loses
hundreds of dollars in customer lifetime value. A False Positive (sending an email
or retention offer to a happy customer) costs pennies. Therefore, high Recall
(catching ~8 out of 10 churners) is the primary optimization goal."

Q3: How did you handle missing values in TotalCharges without data leakage?
A3: "I audited the 11 records with empty spaces in TotalCharges and observed that
all 11 had tenure = 0 months. These were new accounts enrolled before their first
bill. Imputing 0.0 reflects ground-truth physical reality, avoids synthetic
imputation distortion, and prevents deleting valuable new accounts."

Q4: Why Welch's t-test instead of Student's t-test?
A4: "Student's t-test assumes equal population variances (homoscedasticity).
Retained customers and churned customers have unequal group sizes (5,174 vs 1,869)
and unequal variances (std $31.09 vs $24.67). Welch's t-test does not assume
equal variance and adjusts the degrees of freedom via the Welch-Satterthwaite
equation, producing an unbiased p-value."

Q5: How does SQL integrate with Python in this project?
A5: "SQL is utilized for data warehouse aggregations, cohort segmentation, and
window functions (DENSE_RANK) to extract real-time CRM call lists. Python handles
advanced statistical hypothesis testing, data preprocessing pipelines, and
machine learning inference."
